Hill markers

Each hill is drawn as the most important list it is on. The markers for the lists the tile layer already had are unchanged (a circle for a Corbett, a hexagon for a Graham, and so on; hollow means a top, a subsidiary summit on the same hill). The rows marked new are for lists that had no marker. Shown on light, dark and green ground, at 1× and 2×.
